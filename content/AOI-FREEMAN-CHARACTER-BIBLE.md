# AOI FREEMAN — Character Bible

**Role:** Studio Guide and on-screen host of the Founding Creators: AI Anime
Accelerator — Cohort One (Six-Week Program)
**Status:** Original character. Program mascot and teaching persona.
**Owner:** AI GlamTech Empire / Founding Creators studio
**Version:** 1.0 — locked
**Canonical reference image:** `content/assets/aoi-freeman-ref-01.png`

> **The one rule.** Aoi Freeman's design is locked. Nobody — no artist, no AI
> tool, no editor — changes her skin tone, her hair, her palette, or her
> coverage. When a generation comes back wrong, regenerate it. Do not ship it
> and do not "fix it in the caption."

---

## 1. Who She Is

Aoi Freeman is the guide who walks young creators through the studio door.

She is not a student and she is not a sidekick. She is the professional in the
room — the one who already has the portfolio, already knows the vocabulary, and
is turning around to hand it to somebody coming up behind her. Every piece of
content she appears in should feel like that: a capable person taking a
beginner seriously.

**Reads as:** early twenties. An adult mentor, not a teen.
**Carries herself:** composed, still, deliberate. She does not need to be loud
to hold a frame.
**Speaks like:** a working creative who respects the person she's talking to.
Plain words. No baby talk, no hype, no slang performed for effect.

### Her purpose in the brand

She exists to close a specific gap. A lot of Black kids grow up loving anime and
never once see a character built from their own features — and never meet
anybody who tells them the character could be theirs to build. Aoi is the
answer to that. She is proof of concept standing in the frame: an anime
character with dark brown skin and natural coily hair, designed with the same
care and detail the medium gives anyone else.

### Voice rules

- She teaches, she doesn't sell. The program is what she does, not what she
  pitches.
- She uses the real vocabulary — character sheet, silhouette, color script,
  storyboard, key frame — and then defines it. Respecting a kid means giving
  them the actual word.
- She never talks down. No "sweetie," no "little one," no exclamation stacking.
- She is warm but not soft. Encouraging but never flattering.
- Zero profanity, zero innuendo, zero romance plot. This is an education brand.

### Lines that are on-voice

- "You are allowed to be the one who makes it."
- "Inspired is not the same as copied. Learn the difference and you're already a creator."
- "The tool doesn't have taste. You do. That's the part you bring."
- "Start with a silhouette. If I can recognize her as a black shape, you built her right."
- "Nobody's first character is good. Mine wasn't. Make it anyway."

### Lines that are off-voice — never write these

- Anything flirtatious, suggestive, or body-focused.
- "Bestie," "slay," "it's giving," or trend slang with a shelf life.
- "Anyone can do this with AI!" — she never implies the skill is free.
- Anything that promises a scholarship, a job, or an outcome by race.

## 2. Locked Physical Design

### Skin
Rich, deep brown with warm undertones. Smooth anime rendering with soft
edge-light, never flattened or greyed out.

| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Base skin | `#6B4230` |
| Lit / highlight plane | `#8B5A3C` |
| Shadow plane | `#4A2C1E` |
| Neon rim light on skin | `#C084FC` |

**Never:** lighten her, desaturate her, or let a rim light wash her tone out.
If the render comes back lighter than the reference, it is a failed render.

### Hair
Very long, extremely voluminous natural coily-curly hair with defined spiral
curl clumps, falling past her waist and reading almost as a cape. It is
**indigo-violet**, not black, and it carries its own internal light — fine
luminous strands thread through the mass like fiber optic.

| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Hair base | `#1E1B4B` |
| Hair mid tone | `#4338CA` |
| Glow strands / rim | `#A78BFA` |
| Brightest specular | `#E9D5FF` |

Small heart-shaped hair pins sit at her right temple.

**Never:** straighten it, smooth it into a single glossy sheet, shorten it, or
render it as generic anime "wavy" hair. The coil definition is the point.

### Face and eyes
Full lips, soft rounded features, defined brows, long lashes. Expression is
calm and level — a slight knowing set to the mouth rather than a broad smile.

- Iris: amethyst `#9D4EDD` with lilac `#E9D5FF` catchlights
- Heart-shaped drop earrings, glowing violet `#D946EF`

### Wardrobe — the Studio Look

Everything below is canon. This is the outfit she wears in program content.

**Outer layer.** Oversized violet moto-style jacket, patent/leather finish with
a subtle marbled sheen. High popped collar, open front, silver zip hardware,
heart-shaped studs at the collar points and shoulders. A small tech panel is set
into the left upper sleeve with a glowing violet light bar — her studio device.
The jacket falls into a long dramatic coat tail behind her.

**Top.** High-neck black structured top with a fitted bodice, heart-shaped
hardware detail at the chest, fully covered to the collarbone.

**Waist.** Wide layered black belt with silver buckles and heart motifs. A
silver chain swags from the left hip carrying a glowing violet heart charm.

**Skirt.** Layered violet tulle high-low skirt, ruffled tiers, long sweeping
back panel. Structured and full — costume-design volume, not clingy.

**Legs — LOCKED, non-negotiable.** **Opaque black leggings, full coverage,
ankle to waist.** This was a deliberate design decision and it is permanent.
Aoi is a teacher figure in a youth education program. Any render that produces
bare legs, sheer tights, or visible skin between the skirt and the boots is a
failed render and gets discarded.

**Thigh hardware.** Black utility straps around both thighs with silver
heart-shaped rings, plus a small utility pouch with a heart emblem on the left
thigh. Worn over the leggings.

**Boots.** Violet knee-high lace-up boots, patent finish, silver eyelets,
low block heel. Built for standing and working, not for posing.

### Silhouette test
Blacked out to a pure shape she should still be readable by: the enormous coil
halo of hair, the popped collar and long coat tail, the tiered skirt volume, and
the straight column of the leggings and boots. If a render loses any of those
four, the silhouette is wrong.

### Recurring motif
**The heart.** It repeats through her earrings, hair pins, collar studs, belt,
chain charm, thigh rings, and pouch. It is the visual signature of the
Founding Creators studio and it means one thing: this work is made with care.

## 3. Her World

**Setting:** a neon-lit future city at dusk, viewed from a high rooftop
overlook with a dark railing. Violet and magenta skyscraper windows, a sunset
sky of coral, hot pink and deep purple cloud banks, wet reflective ground
catching the city glow.

This is where she teaches. The rooftop reads as the studio balcony — above the
city, looking out at everything a creator could build.

## 5. The Locked Generation Prompt

Paste this as the character block in **every** image or video prompt. Do not
paraphrase it, do not trim it for length, do not let a tool "improve" it.

```
Aoi Freeman, original anime character, cinematic anime illustration, cel-shaded
with soft neon rim lighting.

A confident young Black woman in her early twenties with rich deep brown skin
(warm undertones, never lightened), full lips, soft rounded features, defined
brows, and calm amethyst-violet eyes with lilac catchlights. Her expression is
composed and knowing, not smiling broadly.

HAIR: extremely long, very voluminous natural coily-curly hair with defined
spiral curl clumps falling past her waist like a cape. Deep indigo-violet
(#1E1B4B base, #4338CA mid) with luminous glowing lilac fiber-optic strands
(#A78BFA) threading through it. Small heart-shaped hair pins at her right
temple. NOT straight, NOT smooth, NOT black — coil definition is essential.

OUTFIT: oversized violet patent moto jacket with a high popped collar, open
front, silver zippers, heart-shaped studs, and a glowing violet tech light-bar
panel on the left upper sleeve, falling into a long dramatic coat tail. High-neck
black structured bodice top, fully covered to the collarbone, with heart-shaped
hardware. Wide layered black belt with silver buckles and heart motifs. Silver
hip chain with a glowing violet heart charm. Layered violet tulle high-low
ruffled skirt with a long sweeping back panel.

LEGS: OPAQUE BLACK FULL-LENGTH LEGGINGS, complete coverage from waist to ankle.
Black thigh straps with silver heart-shaped rings worn OVER the leggings, and a
small heart-emblem utility pouch on the left thigh. Violet knee-high lace-up
patent boots with silver eyelets and a low block heel.

MODEST, FULLY COVERED, PROFESSIONAL. She is a teacher and mentor figure. No bare
legs, no sheer fabric, no exposed midriff, no cleavage, no suggestive posing.

SETTING: neon-lit futuristic city rooftop at dusk, dark railing, violet and
magenta skyscraper windows, sunset sky of coral and hot pink and deep purple
clouds, wet reflective ground.

PALETTE: violet, magenta, indigo, neon purple. Heart motifs repeated throughout.

Style: high-quality modern anime key visual, detailed line work, dramatic
lighting, 8k.
```

### Negative prompt — always include

```
lightened skin, pale skin, whitewashed, straight hair, smooth glossy hair,
short hair, black hair, bare legs, sheer tights, fishnets, exposed thighs,
crop top, midriff, cleavage, low neckline, revealing, suggestive pose,
swimwear, child, western cartoon style, photorealistic, extra limbs,
deformed hands, text, watermark, logo
```

### Render QC checklist

Before any image of Aoi ships, check all seven:

1. Skin is deep brown and matches the reference. Not lightened.
2. Hair is long, coily, indigo-violet, with visible curl definition and glow.
3. Legs are fully covered in opaque black leggings.
4. Neckline is high and closed. Nothing revealing.
5. Heart motifs are present somewhere on the outfit.
6. Palette is violet/magenta. No stray colors.
7. Expression is composed and dignified.

Any single failure means regenerate. Seven for seven or it doesn't post.

## 6. Consistency Across a Series

Anime characters drift when you generate them one at a time. To keep her the
same person across fifteen days of content:

- **Always start from the reference image** using image-to-image, character
  reference, or a style-reference feature where the tool supports it.
- **Change one variable at a time.** Change the camera angle or the expression,
  not both plus the setting.
- **Keep the outfit fixed.** Aoi has one canonical look. Consistency is what
  makes her a character instead of a stock illustration.
- **Save every approved render** into `content/assets/` with a dated filename.
  Approved renders become future references, which tightens consistency as the
  library grows.
- **Reuse an approved render** rather than rolling a new one when the shot is
  close enough. A repeated image is better than an off-model one.

## 7. Attribution and Honesty

Aoi Freeman is an original character created for this program. She is not from
any existing anime, and content should never imply a connection to a
copyrighted franchise. This models exactly what the accelerator teaches:

> **Be inspired. Don't copy. Build your own universe.**

Where student work appears alongside her, follow the program's photo, video,
and work-display permissions. Students whose families decline promotional use
still participate fully — their work simply doesn't appear in content.
