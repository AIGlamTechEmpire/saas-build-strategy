## The core belief

> **AI is the last phase, not the first.**

Most people in this market sell AI as step one. This methodology refuses that. It starts
with a human conversation, diagnoses the business, fixes the leaks, builds the operating
procedures — and only then installs AI. That refusal is the positioning.

---

## The three-word diagnostic

Everything runs on three questions, asked in this order.

### 1. BLEEDING — where is the business losing?

Where is time, money, or energy leaking out. Usually a task the owner is personally doing
that eats the hours they should be spending on revenue.

**The named test:** *"What happens to this business if you get sick for three days, or go
on vacation? Does it stop?"*

If the business stops when the owner stops, that is the bleed. Nothing else needs to be
proven.

### 2. FEEDING — what is bringing revenue in?

Is there a consistent flow of clients arriving — from social, paid ads, referrals,
speaking, anywhere. Feeding is not "am I busy." Feeding is "does new revenue arrive
without me hoping."

**The rule:** if there is no consistent inbound and the goal is not being hit, the business
is not feeding. Full stop.

### 3. NEEDING — what does it need to start feeding?

Work backward from the goal.

```
12-month goal  ÷ 12  =  monthly target
monthly target  vs.  actual monthly revenue  =  the gap
the gap  →  what has to happen in the NEXT 30 DAYS
```

The 30-day plan is never "grow the business." It is a named list of revenue activities —
speaking engagements, Chamber of Commerce, specific outreach — plus the SOPs that free up
the hours to do them.

## The delivery sequence

The order is the product. It does not get skipped.

| # | Phase | What happens | Human or AI |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Conversation** | Real human intake. What does your day look like. What are you making daily. What is the 12-month goal. | **Human only** |
| 2 | **Brain** | Everything from the conversation goes into the Brain Builder skill so the system knows the business | AI-assisted |
| 3 | **Bleed diagnostic** | Where is time and money leaking. Scored, ranked by severity. Spreadsheet-backed. | AI-assisted |
| 4 | **Feed audit** | Is revenue arriving consistently, and from where | AI-assisted |
| 5 | **The 30-day plan** | Goal ÷ 12 → the gap → the named revenue activities for the next 30 days | AI-assisted |
| 6 | **SOPs** | Written procedures around the biggest bleeds, so the work stops depending on the owner | AI-assisted |
| 7 | **Automation** | Take the worst task off the table. May not need AI at all — a plain automation often wins. | AI-assisted |
| 8 | **AI workforce** | Only now. The command center, the agent team. | **AI — last** |
