## How to Use This Document

When the AI is generating persuasion content (sales pages, sales emails, objection-handling, content marketing), it should:
1. Check the Objection-to-Proof Mapping to find the right proof for the moment.
2. Use exact quotes, never paraphrase testimonials.
3. Cite attribution accurately (real names, real roles).
4. Default to the Top 10 testimonials when in doubt.
5. Pull case studies when a longer narrative is needed.
6. Never invent or fabricate proof. If the right proof isn't here, ask the user.

## Drafting notes

- Quote testimonials VERBATIM. Even typos. Even awkward phrasing. Real wording is more credible than polished.
- The objection-to-proof mapping is what makes this doc actively useful. Don't skip it.
- If the user has fewer than 10 testimonials, do 5 well rather than padding to 10.
- Case studies should be SPECIFIC. Avoid "we helped a customer achieve great results." Use names, numbers, and specifics.
- If pulling from a testimonials page, capture every piece of attribution available (name, title, company, photo URL if relevant).
- Mark `[TO REFINE]` for proof points that need verification.
