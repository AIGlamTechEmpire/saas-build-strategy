# Schema: 01 Company DNA

This is the markdown structure for the Company DNA document. Draft the doc with these sections in this order. Use the user's actual words wherever possible.

## Output filename
`01_Company_DNA.md`

## Required structure

```markdown
# Company DNA — [BUSINESS NAME]

> The identity layer. Who we are, why we exist, what we stand for. Every AI employee in this business pulls from this doc on every task.

---

## Identity

**🟢 Business name:** [Name]

**🟢 What we do (1 sentence):** [Plain English description, in user's voice]

**🟢 Who we serve (1 sentence):** [Specific ICP description]

**🟢 Why we exist (1 sentence):** [The bigger purpose beyond profit]

---

## Origin Story

**🟢 The founding story:**
[2-4 paragraphs. The factual origin: who founded it, when, how. Then the emotional layer: why they started, what they were angry about, what they wanted to fix. Use the founder's voice. Pull direct quotes if available.]

**🟢 The personal connection:**
[1-2 paragraphs. Why does THIS founder care about THIS problem? What's their personal stake?]

---

## Mission and Vision

**🟢 Mission (one sentence):**
[The plain-English mission, not corporate-speak. What this business is trying to do in the world.]

**🟡 10-year vision:**
[If captured. What does success look like in 10 years? Skip if not provided.]

---

## Values

**🟢 Core values (3 to 5):**

For each value, format as:

- **[Value name]:** [One sentence definition in plain English. What does this look like in practice?]

Avoid generic values (integrity, excellence, innovation). Push for specific, actionable values that reflect this business's actual operating choices.

---

## Positioning

**🟢 Category we play in:**
[The category this business competes in. Use the user's framing — sometimes the category they say they're in is different from the category an outsider would assign.]

**🟢 What makes us different:**
[3-5 specific differentiators. Not "we care more." Specific approaches, methodologies, or beliefs that set this business apart.]

**🟢 Top competitors:**
List 2-4 named competitors and one sentence on how this business differs from each:

- **[Competitor name]:** [How we're different]

---

## Personality

**🟢 Three adjectives that describe our personality:**
1. [Adjective 1]
2. [Adjective 2]
3. [Adjective 3]

**🟢 What we sound like in one sentence:**
[A vivid one-liner. Example: "Like the smartest friend in your industry, but without the lecture."]

**🟡 Brand archetype (if useful):**
[Optional. The Hero, the Sage, the Outlaw, etc. Skip if not naturally fitting.]

---

## What We Stand Against

**🟡 The thing we will not do:**
[What does this business reject? What approach does the founder think is wrong? What would they never do, even if it was profitable?]

---

## How to Use This Document

This document is the foundation. Every other Brain document references back to the identity captured here. When in doubt about a decision, voice choice, or positioning question, return to this file.
```

## Drafting notes

- Lead with the most truthful version, not the marketing version. The Brain is internal-facing.
- If a section is sparse, mark `[TO REFINE: brief note]` and move on.
- Origin Story is the soul of this doc. Spend time on it.
- Avoid generic content. If you find yourself writing "We are passionate about delivering exceptional value to our customers," delete it and try again with specifics.
- Direct quotes from the founder are gold. Use them.
