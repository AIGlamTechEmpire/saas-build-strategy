## How to Use This Document

Every AI-generated piece of content should be checked against this doc:
1. Does it match the 3 voice adjectives?
2. Does it follow the DO rules and avoid the DON'T rules?
3. Does it contain any banned words or AI tells?
4. Could one of the greatest hits samples sit next to this output and feel like the same writer?

If the answer to any of these is no, the AI should iterate. If the answer to all is yes, ship it.

## Drafting notes

- The greatest hits samples are the single most important section. Pull at least 5, ideally 8-10, from the user's real writing.
- Make the banned words list SPECIFIC. Vague rules like "avoid jargon" don't help an AI. "Never use 'leverage' or 'synergy'" does.
- The 3 voice adjectives should feel like THIS brand specifically. If they could describe any brand, they're not specific enough.
- Pull the AI tells from the user's stated preferences. If they didn't mention any in the interview, use the defaults listed above.
- If the user has visibly different voices for different contexts, capture that in Voice Variation. If their voice is consistent, skip that section.
