# Schema: 02 ICP Profile

This is the markdown structure for the ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) document. Capture the way your ideal customer actually thinks, talks, and decides.

## Output filename
`02_ICP_Profile.md`

## Required structure

```markdown
# ICP Profile — [BUSINESS NAME]

> Who we serve. How they think. What they need. Every piece of marketing, sales, and customer-facing content this business produces should be written FOR this person.

---

## Demographics

**🟢 Primary ICP description (1-2 sentences):**
[Specific: title/role, industry, stage of business or life, what makes them recognizable in 10 seconds.]

**🟢 Demographic specifics:**
- **Title or role:** [Specific titles or roles]
- **Industry:** [Industry or industries]
- **Stage:** [Business stage, life stage, career stage]
- **Income or revenue range:** [If relevant]
- **Geography:** [If geographically constrained]
- **Other identifiers:** [Anything else that helps recognize this ICP]

**🟡 Secondary ICPs:**
[If the business serves more than one ICP, describe the others briefly. Otherwise skip.]

---

## Psychographics

**🟢 The emotional state when they find us:**
[1-2 paragraphs. What are they feeling? Frustrated, burned out, desperate, curious, ambitious? What's the moment that made them search for a solution?]

**🟢 Top 3 pains in their own words:**
1. "[Direct quote or close paraphrase of pain as ICP would say it]"
2. "[Pain 2]"
3. "[Pain 3]"

**🟢 Top 3 desires in their own words:**
1. "[Direct quote or close paraphrase of desire]"
2. "[Desire 2]"
3. "[Desire 3]"

**🟢 What's keeping them up at night:**
[1-2 sentences. The fear or anxiety that pushes them to action.]

---

## Beliefs

**🟢 What they currently believe about [our category]:**
[2-3 sentences. The default beliefs they hold when they show up. Sometimes these are wrong. That's the gap to close.]

**🟢 What they need to believe to buy:**
[2-3 sentences. The shift in belief required before they can say yes.]

**🟢 Where they've been burned before:**
[1-2 paragraphs. What past experience makes them skeptical? Who or what failed them before? This shapes how trust gets built.]

**🟡 Common objections:**
- "[Objection 1]" → [How we typically respond]
- "[Objection 2]" → [How we typically respond]
- "[Objection 3]" → [How we typically respond]

---

## Vocabulary

**🟢 Words and phrases they use:**
[5-10 terms or phrases that are insider language for this ICP. These should appear in marketing copy that resonates.]

**🟢 Words they roll their eyes at:**
[5-10 phrases that make this ICP think "this person doesn't get me." These should NEVER appear in marketing copy. Often corporate jargon, generic motivation, or industry clichés.]

**🟡 Inside jokes or references:**
[Cultural references, memes, or shared knowledge this ICP would recognize. Skip if not applicable.]

---

## Behavior

**🟡 Where they spend their attention:**
- **Content platforms:** [Where they consume — podcasts, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, newsletters]
- **Communities:** [Where they hang out — specific groups, conferences, forums]
- **Decision-influencers:** [Who they listen to — specific names if known]

**🟡 How they make buying decisions:**
[1-2 paragraphs. Fast and gut? Slow and analytical? Who do they consult? What proof do they need?]

---

## Hero Customer (Example of ICP)

**🟡 A specific named example of this ICP:**
[2-3 paragraphs. One real customer who perfectly represents the ICP. Their before-state, what changed, and where they are now. Use their words wherever possible. This becomes a reference example for the AI.]

---

## How to Use This Document

When drafting marketing, sales, or content for this business, the AI should:
1. Write FOR this specific person, not a generic audience.
2. Use their vocabulary, avoid their pet peeves.
3. Address their actual pain and desire, not the surface version.
4. Acknowledge their past disappointments before pitching a solution.
```

## Drafting notes

- The ICP doc lives or dies on specificity. Generic ICPs produce generic marketing.
- Use direct quotes from sales call transcripts and customer surveys wherever possible.
- The "Vocabulary" section is gold for matching how the ICP actually talks.
- If the user serves multiple ICPs that are genuinely different (e.g., agencies AND solo founders), call that out. Don't smush them together.
- Mark `[TO REFINE]` for any pain/belief/objection that feels generic or unsupported.
