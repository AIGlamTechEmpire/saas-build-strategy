## Offer 1: Growth Audit

**🟢 Name and tagline:**
Growth Audit. *Find the leak before you scale the spend.*

**🟢 Price and structure:**
$2,500 one-time. Delivered within 14 days of payment. No retainer commitment.

**🟢 Who it's for:**
Founders doing $1M+ who suspect their unit economics are off but can't quite pin down where. Often used as a "try before you buy" before committing to the full retainer.

**🟢 The promise:**
A specific, prioritized list of the 5-7 highest-leverage fixes in your paid and email setup, each one quantified with projected impact on contribution margin.

**🟢 What's included:**
- Full audit of Meta Ads, Google Ads, and Klaviyo
- Contribution margin model built from your actual data
- Identification of the top 3 leaks in current setup
- Prioritized fix list with effort vs. impact scoring
- 60-minute walkthrough call with Sarah
- Written report you can keep regardless of next steps

**🟢 What's NOT included:**
- Execution of the fixes (that's the Retainer)
- Creative production
- Ongoing optimization

**🟢 Hero customer story:**
**Maya Chen** (7-figure DTC skincare brand) ran the Growth Audit before committing to the Retainer. The audit identified $14K/month in wasted ad spend on a campaign that had been running on autopilot for 8 months. The audit paid for itself in the first 5 days of fixes.

**🟡 Common objections + responses:**
- *"Can't you just give me this for free as a sales pitch?"* → No. The Audit is real work. Anything we'd give away for free wouldn't be worth running.
- *"What if I don't want the Retainer after?"* → You don't have to. The Audit is standalone. ~30% of audit clients don't move to retainer.

**🟡 Where this offer fits in the journey:**
Entry point. Often a feeder into the Retainer. Sometimes a one-off for brands not ready for full agency engagement.

## Offer 2: Paid + Email Retainer

**🟢 Name and tagline:**
Paid + Email Retainer. *Real hands on the account. Real eyes on the numbers. Real growth that doesn't break your margins.*

**🟢 Price and structure:**
$6,500 base retainer per month, plus 8% of monthly ad spend over $50K/month. No long-term contract. Month-to-month, cancel anytime with 30 days notice.

**🟢 Who it's for:**
Brands doing $1M to $20M committed to scaling profitably. Must have at least 12 months of ad spend history. Must be willing to share contribution margin data (otherwise we can't do our job).

**🟢 The promise:**
Hands-on management of paid acquisition (Meta, Google, sometimes TikTok) and email retention (Klaviyo) by a dedicated team. Monthly reporting that leads with contribution margin, not ROAS. Direct access to a strategist. Quarterly reviews with Sarah.

**🟢 What's included:**
- Dedicated growth strategist (your account lead)
- Creative producer for ad assets and email design
- Weekly campaign management and optimization
- Bi-weekly check-in call with your strategist
- Monthly reporting (contribution margin first, then channel detail)
- Quarterly strategic review with Sarah
- Slack channel for fast questions
- Klaviyo flow audit and optimization
- Creative testing budget recommendations

**🟢 What's NOT included:**
- Website or landing page builds
- Organic social media management
- PR or influencer outreach
- SMS marketing (we partner with Postscript specialists for this if needed)
- The ad spend itself (paid directly to the platforms by you)

**🟢 Hero customer story:**
**Maya Chen** (continued from above) moved to the Retainer after the Audit. Six months in: ROAS up from 1.8 to 2.4, contribution margin up 11 percentage points, brand grew 38% YoY without raising prices. *"I stopped feeling like I was the smartest person in the room about my own marketing. Finally."*

**🟡 Common objections + responses:**
- *"$6,500 base feels high."* → For brands under $1M, it is. For brands $1M-$20M, it's the right price point. We're priced for our actual market.
- *"What if my spend grows fast? The 8% gets expensive."* → That's by design. We earn more when we scale your account profitably. Our incentives align with yours.
- *"Why month-to-month?"* → Because we'd rather earn the relationship than enforce it.

**🟡 Where this offer fits in the journey:**
Core flagship. Most clients enter here (with or without the Audit first). Average client tenure: 18 months.

## Offer 3: Quarterly Strategy Sessions

**🟢 Name and tagline:**
Quarterly Strategy Sessions. *Outside perspective for in-house teams.*

**🟢 Price and structure:**
$4,000 per quarter ($16,000 annually). 90-day engagements with renewal optional.

**🟢 Who it's for:**
Brands doing $5M+ that have built in-house growth teams but want quarterly outside review and strategic input. Often used by brands who've outgrown agency management but still want senior strategic guidance.

**🟢 The promise:**
A full quarterly deep-dive into your paid and email performance, a fresh outside perspective on what's working and what isn't, and a strategic roadmap for the next 90 days. Delivered as a written report plus a 2-hour working session.

**🟢 What's included:**
- Quarterly audit of paid and email performance
- Written strategic report (~15 pages)
- 2-hour live strategy session with Sarah
- One follow-up call mid-quarter to track progress
- Slack access for the quarter

**🟢 What's NOT included:**
- Execution (your in-house team owns this)
- Daily account management
- Creative production

**🟡 Hero customer story:**
Cross-channel apparel brand at $14M annual revenue. In-house growth team of 3. Hired Westwood for quarterly sessions to challenge their assumptions and bring outside benchmarks. Two quarters in: identified a Klaviyo flow that was actively losing them money and helped restructure their attribution model.

**🟡 Where this offer fits in the journey:**
For brands that have graduated from the Retainer (or never needed it). Top of the ascension path.

## Ascension Path

**🟢 The customer journey through offers:**

```
Discovery: Found us via founder referral, podcast, or content
       ↓
Optional entry: Growth Audit ($2,500 one-time)
       ↓
Core engagement: Paid + Email Retainer ($6,500/mo + 8% over $50K spend)
       ↓
Long-term partnership: Either continue retainer indefinitely (most common)
       OR
       Graduate to Quarterly Strategy Sessions ($4K/quarter) once they've built in-house team
```

**🟡 Typical timing between offers:**
Audit → Retainer: Within 30 days for the ~70% who convert. Retainer → Quarterly: 18-36 months in, typically when the client has hired 2+ in-house growth people.

**🟡 Why customers ascend:**
The Audit proves we can find leaks. The Retainer proves we can fix them at scale. The Quarterly proves we can stay strategically valuable even after the client has internalized the work.

## Refund and Guarantee Policy

**🟢 Growth Audit:**
If the audit doesn't surface at least 3 actionable, quantified opportunities, full refund. We've issued this refund twice in 5 years.

**🟢 Retainer:**
No long-term contract. Month-to-month. Cancel with 30 days notice. No prorated refunds within a month, but no clawback either.

**🟢 Quarterly Strategy:**
Pay quarterly upfront. If the first quarter doesn't deliver value, refund of the remaining quarters in the engagement.

**🟢 The philosophy:**
We're priced to be sustainable, not maximally extractive. Refunds happen when we don't deliver. We don't fight them.
