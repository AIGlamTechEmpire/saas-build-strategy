## Hero Case Studies

### Case Study: Maya Chen — From 1.8 ROAS to 38% YoY Growth

**🟢 Background:**
Maya inherited a family skincare brand and modernized it. Hit $4M in revenue in 3 years. Had fired two previous agencies. Was running a 1.8 ROAS and didn't know her contribution margin.

**🟢 The before-state:**
Spending $80K/month on Meta with a "fine" ROAS that wasn't actually fine. Klaviyo flows hadn't been updated in 14 months. The previous agency had been running 5 ads to the same audiences for 6 months. Maya was working 60-hour weeks trying to figure out why revenue grew but profit didn't.

**🟢 The solution:**
Started with the Growth Audit. Identified $14K/month in wasted spend on a campaign nobody was monitoring, a Klaviyo flow that was actively losing money, and an attribution window that was inflating ROAS by ~30%. Moved to the Retainer in week 3.

**🟢 The transformation:**
Six months in: ROAS up from 1.8 to 2.4, contribution margin up 11 percentage points, brand grew 38% YoY. Maya now reviews contribution margin weekly. Hired a Director of Operations using the freed-up margin.

**🟢 The quotable line:**
*"I stopped feeling like I was the smartest person in the room about my own marketing. Finally."*

**🟡 What this case proves:**
That the right diagnostic (Audit) plus the right execution (Retainer) compounds. That contribution margin is the right headline metric. That a founder doesn't have to choose between growth and profit.

### Case Study: Priya M. — The $42K Klaviyo Fix

**🟢 Background:**
Founder of a $3M supplements brand. Inherited her email setup from a freelancer. Didn't know what was in her Klaviyo flows.

**🟢 The before-state:**
A welcome flow that had been "optimized" by a previous freelancer. Sarah audited it and found that the flow was over-discounting first-time buyers in a way that destroyed first-purchase contribution margin AND lowered LTV (because discount-trained buyers don't repeat at full price).

**🟢 The solution:**
Audit identified the flow. Restructured it within the Audit deliverable (rare exception, but the fix was clear-cut). The flow went from -8% contribution margin on first purchase to +24%.

**🟢 The transformation:**
$42K in additional contribution margin over the following 90 days. The Audit cost $2,500. ROI: 17x in 90 days.

**🟢 The quotable line:**
*"Sarah looked at my Klaviyo and said 'this flow is actively losing you money.' She was right."*

**🟡 What this case proves:**
That email retention is where most DTC brands are leaking. That an Audit can pay for itself many times over. That sometimes the highest-leverage fix is turning something OFF.
