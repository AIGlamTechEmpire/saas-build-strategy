# Operating Principles — Westwood Marketing

> How we work. How we decide. What we say YES to and what we say NO to. The standards and rhythms that govern this business.

---

## Decision-Making Principles

**🟢 The lenses we use when stuck on a hard call:**

- **Math over vibes.** When two paths are debatable, run the math. The path with better contribution margin math wins.

- **Earn the relationship, don't enforce it.** Never lock a client in with contracts they want to leave. If we have to enforce, we've already failed.

- **Say the hard thing.** When a client is going to lose, tell them. Even if it costs us the engagement. Founders deserve the truth.

- **Boring beats clever.** When deciding between a flashy campaign and an unsexy one with better margins, the unsexy one wins.

- **Stay in scope.** We do paid acquisition and email retention for DTC e-commerce. We don't expand outside that. Every time we've tried, we've underperformed.

## Work Ownership Map (The 4Ds)

> Where each function in Westwood currently sits across the 4Ds, and where it's heading. Every AI employee built here references this map.

**🟢 The 4Ds (for reference):**

- **Doing** — You execute the work yourself
- **Deciding** — You don't execute but every instance needs your judgment
- **Delegating** — A team member or AI employee owns it, runs without you
- **Designing** — You're out of the work, architecting the system

**🟢 Current state of each function:**

| Function | Owner | Current Mode | Target Mode | Notes |
|----------|-------|---------------|--------------|-------|
| Marketing | Sarah | Deciding | Delegating | Currently Sarah approves every outbound. Moving to AI-drafted, Sarah-reviewed by Q2. |
| Sales | Sarah | Deciding | Delegating | Sales calls stay Sarah-led (Designing). Follow-up and proposal generation moving to Delegating. |
| Product / Delivery (account management) | Strategists | Delegating | Delegating | Already well-distributed. Holding here. |
| Creative Production | Creative team | Delegating | Delegating | In-house creative team owns this. Stable. |
| Customer Success | Strategists | Delegating | Delegating | Strategists own client relationships. AI assists with reporting and prep. |
| Operations | Sarah + ops contractor | Deciding | Delegating | Sarah still in the loop too much. AI scheduling, billing automation, internal reporting moving to Delegating. |
| Finance | External CPA + Sarah | Delegating | Delegating | CPA owns the work. Sarah reviews monthly. Stable. |
| Hiring | Sarah | Designing | Designing | Sarah owns this permanently. Never delegating. |

**🟢 Functions where Sarah stays at Designing permanently:**

- **Hiring decisions** — Sarah personally interviews and decides every team hire. Never delegating.
- **Brand voice approval** — All major brand-voice decisions go through Sarah. AI drafts, Sarah approves.
- **Pricing decisions** — Sarah personally approves any pricing change, new package, or major discount.
- **Client fit decisions** — Sarah personally approves taking on or turning down any new client.

**🟡 12-week priority order (for AI workforce build):**

1. Weeks 2-3: **Marketing** function. Highest volume of repetitive work (ad creative iterations, A/B test ideas, copy variations). Highest ROI on AI deployment.
2. Weeks 4-5: **Sales** function. Sales follow-up email automation, proposal generation. Biggest time sink for Sarah personally.
3. Weeks 6-7: **Operations** function. Internal reporting, scheduling, billing reconciliation. Frees Sarah from non-strategic ops work.
4. Weeks 8-9: **Customer Success** assistive AI for strategists. Report drafting, client prep, meeting summaries.
5. Weeks 10-12: **Cross-function workforce** — connecting the AI employees across functions so they hand work to each other.

## Communication Standards

**🟢 Response times:**
- **Client Slack messages:** Within 4 business hours.
- **Client emails:** Within 24 business hours.
- **Inbound prospect inquiries:** Within 24 hours.
- **Urgent campaign issues:** Within 1 hour during business hours.

**🟢 Channel norms:**
- **Slack:** Primary client communication. Fast back-and-forth, daily questions, urgent issues.
- **Email:** Monthly reports, formal updates, anything that needs a paper trail.
- **Zoom:** Bi-weekly check-ins (30 min), quarterly reviews (60-90 min).
- **In-person:** Annual strategic sessions for clients $5M+ if they want it.

**🟢 Tone for different contexts:**
- **With clients:** Direct, warm, peer-to-peer. Never "vendor energy."
- **With prospects:** Same warmth, more candid. We'll tell a prospect they're not a fit faster than we'll try to close them.
- **With team:** Direct, technical. Numbers first.
- **In public/marketing:** Confident but understated. No hype.

## Quality Standards

**🟢 What "good enough to ship" means:**
For client-facing work: every number is verifiable, every recommendation traces back to data the client can audit themselves, every assertion can be defended in a quarterly review. We don't ship anything we couldn't justify in front of the client's CFO.

For internal work: rough drafts ship, polish happens through iteration. We don't perfect internal docs.

**🟢 Specific quality bars:**

- **Monthly client reports:** One-page email leading with contribution margin. Detail in linked dashboard for those who want it. No 30-slide decks.
- **Audit reports:** Written, 8-12 pages, every claim backed by a screenshot or data export.
- **Strategic recommendations:** Always quantified. "This change will likely improve contribution margin by 4-7 points over 60 days."
- **Sales pages and marketing content:** Production-ready, tested, every claim defensible.
- **Internal SOPs:** Rough is fine. Improved as edge cases hit.

## What We Say YES To

**🟢 Things we do that other agencies don't:**

- **We send month-to-month contracts.** No 6-month or 12-month minimums.
- **We turn down ~40% of inbound inquiries.** Misfits get a polite no and a referral.
- **We lead client reports with contribution margin, not ROAS.**
- **We hand-build creative.** No outsourced creative shops.
- **The founder is in every client kickoff and quarterly review.** Sarah doesn't just sell.
- **We tell clients to spend less on us when their margin doesn't support it.**
- **We refer clients to competitors when we're not the right fit.**

---

## What We Say NO To

**🟢 Things we won't do even when the money is good:**

- **Long-term contracts.** Period.
- **ROAS guarantees.** Any agency that gives them is lying.
- **Brand campaigns with no measurable outcome.**
- **Working with brands whose unit economics don't work at scale.** If CAC > LTV at current pricing, we say so and decline.
- **Services we don't deliver well** (websites, organic social, PR, influencer outreach, SMS).
- **Generic strategy decks without data.**
- **AI-generated ad copy without human creative direction.** We use AI as a tool, not a replacement.
- **Pretending to be a generalist agency to win bigger contracts.**
- **Adding "AI" to our pitch as a buzzword.** If AI improves the work, we use it silently.
- **Sub-$1M revenue brands** (with rare exceptions). They can't afford us in a way that's sustainable for them.

## Business Rhythms

**🟡 Weekly cadence:**
- **Monday:** Team standup (45 min). Each strategist reviews their accounts, flags issues.
- **Tuesday & Thursday:** Client work, deep focus blocks.
- **Wednesday:** Creative production day.
- **Friday:** Internal review and the following week's planning.

**🟡 Monthly cadence:**
- **First week of month:** Monthly reports go out to all clients.
- **Second week:** Bi-weekly client check-ins (rotation).
- **Last week:** Sarah's review of all account performance, flag any accounts at risk.

**🟡 Quarterly cadence:**
- **Quarterly reviews with all retainer clients** (60-90 min).
- **Internal team strategic review:** What's working, what's not, who to fire (clients) or hire (team).
- **Pricing review:** Are retainer prices still right? Update if needed.

**🟡 Annual cadence:**
- **Annual roadmap:** New services to test, services to retire.
- **Team retreat:** 3 days, somewhere good.
- **Client satisfaction survey:** Anonymous, brutal, useful.

## Customer Service Philosophy

**🟢 How we handle an unhappy client:**
We listen first. We acknowledge what went wrong without defending. We solve the immediate problem (credit, refund, fix) before explaining. If we screwed up, we say so plainly and make it right.

**🟢 What we will never say to a client:**
- "Per our contract..." (We don't have those kinds of contracts.)
- "That's not in scope." (If it's small, we just do it. If it's big, we explain why it'd be a separate engagement.)
- "I'll have to escalate this." (Sarah's job is to remove escalation friction.)
- "We don't typically..."
- Any phrase that makes the client feel like a transaction.

**🟡 Service standards:**
- **Credits:** Generous. If we missed something, we credit hours back without making the client ask.
- **Refunds:** Audits refund if we don't deliver the promised number of opportunities. Retainers refund prorated only if WE walk away mid-month (rare).
- **Disputes:** Solve first, explain second.

## Hiring and Team Philosophy

**🟢 What we look for in team members:**
- DTC e-commerce experience, ideally in-house (not just agency)
- Demonstrable ability to read a P&L
- Direct communication style; can give and receive hard feedback
- Comfortable being wrong in public, doesn't hide mistakes

**🟢 How we treat people:**
- Salaried with performance bonuses tied to client retention and margin improvement (not revenue or ROAS).
- 4-day work week (Friday is planning/admin, not client-facing).
- Annual learning stipend ($2K).
- Equity-light, salary-strong. We want operators, not lottery-ticket holders.

**🟢 What we won't tolerate:**
- Hiding mistakes from clients.
- Treating clients as transactions.
- Showing up to client meetings unprepared.
- Defaulting to "the client should know what they want" when the client is confused.

## Money and Pricing Principles

**🟢 How we think about pricing:**
We're priced to be sustainable for the team, profitable for the agency, and accretive for the client. If a client can't make our retainer ROI-positive in 90 days, our pricing is wrong for them.

**🟡 When we discount:**
- Almost never. Discounts erode the perception of value.
- One exception: a client experiencing a temporary cash crunch can request a 60-day pause at half rate, once per relationship.

**🟡 When we walk away from money:**
- Clients whose unit economics don't work even with our help.
- Clients who treat the team disrespectfully (one warning, then we exit).
- Clients who demand services we don't offer.
- Brands in categories we choose not to serve (CBD, supplements with regulatory risk, etc.).

## Marketing and Sales Principles

**🟡 How we sell:**
Long sales cycle (3-8 weeks). Founder-to-founder conversations. We educate during the sales process and walk away cleanly if there's no fit. The Audit functions as a soft entry — most retainer clients pass through an Audit first.

**🟡 What we will never do in marketing:**
- Manufactured countdown timers or fake scarcity.
- "Limited spots" claims when there are clearly more available.
- Pretending to be bigger than we are (8-person agency, not 80).
- Borrowed credibility (logos of brands we haven't actually worked with).
- Comparison ads against named competitors.
