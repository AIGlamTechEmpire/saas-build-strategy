# OPP Plays — Other People's People

**Source:** Alicia Lyttle, AI Consultant Bonus OPP Collection. Purchased training material,
stored here for Casandra's own use.

**What it is:** 50 outreach plays, numbered #101–#150. Each one is a prompt, not an email.

**The pattern every play follows:**

```
ONE AUDIENCE  →  ONE DECISION MAKER  →  ONE OFFER  →  EMAIL SENT
```

The core idea: *don't build the room — find the person who already owns the room.* Instead of
growing an audience from scratch, identify an organization that has already gathered the exact
people you are qualified to train, and get invited to train them.

**The 50 plays cluster into five families:**

| Plays | Family | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 101–120 | Software / platform customer bases | SaaS communities, CRM ecosystems, HR tech, fintech, e-commerce platforms, dev communities |
| 121–130 | Corporate internal groups | ERGs (women's, Black employee network, veterans, young professionals), sales teams, leadership development, supplier diversity |
| 131–136 | Professional firms' client bases | Accounting firms, law firms, insurance brokers, HR consultants, marketing agencies |
| 137–146 | Existing communities and audiences | Masterminds, Skool, Facebook groups, LinkedIn communities, Meetups, podcasts, newsletters, conferences |
| 147–150 | Partner and alumni networks | Franchisors, vendor ecosystems, certification alumni, distribution partners |

**Why this matters for the Pinky Business Brain Revenue System**

In the Bleeding / Feeding / Needing framework, this is the **FEEDING** engine — the repeatable
mechanism that brings revenue in. At a $3,500/hr rate, a single booked training of ~4 hours is
roughly $14,000. Casandra needs about 18 billable hours a month to hit her $62,500 target.

**One booked play covers roughly a quarter of a month's goal.**

That makes this the highest-value automation target in the entire business — higher than
content, higher than video, higher than the agent build itself.
