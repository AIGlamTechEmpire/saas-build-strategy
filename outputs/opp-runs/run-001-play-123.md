## The offer — one low-friction live training

> ### "Where Your Work Is Bleeding: Find the 10 Hours AI Should Be Doing For You"
> **60 minutes. Live. Free to the chapter.**

**Three outcomes members leave with:**

1. **A mapped list of their own repeatable tasks**, with hours per month attached — built live,
   on their real work, not a hypothetical
2. **A scored priority** showing which single task to automate first, and why the obvious one is
   usually wrong
3. **One working automation**, built on the call, that runs after they close the laptop

**Why this offer and not a talk.** A talk gets applause. A working session gets a member who has
a thing that works and tells the chapter about it. It is also a live demonstration of your paid
diagnostic — the free hour *is* the method, at small scale. That is the natural bridge to the
paid engagement without ever pitching one.

**Do not mention pricing in the first email.** The play is explicit and it is right.
